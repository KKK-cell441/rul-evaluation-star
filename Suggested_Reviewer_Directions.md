# Suggested Reviewer Directions

For the Measurement submission, prefer reviewers with expertise in:

- Prognostics methodology
- Reliability evaluation
- Machine health monitoring
- Reproducibility and benchmarking

Avoid reviewers whose primary focus is purely model innovation (new CNN, Transformer, or attention architectures).

Suggested areas for the online submission form:

- Condition monitoring and fault diagnosis
- Remaining useful life prediction and prognostics
- Evaluation methodology and reproducibility
- Reliability of measurement results
