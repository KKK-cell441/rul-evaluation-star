\section*{Coding Rules}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Piecewise80}: the paper uses piecewise linear RUL labels with an 80\% plateau.
\item \textbf{RandomSplit}: the paper uses random time-step splitting without per-bearing holdout.
\item \textbf{ConstantBaseline}: the paper reports a constant predictor or equivalent trivial baseline.
\end{enumerate}
